Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what the WordByWord Translate extension for Chrome and Microsoft Edge does with your data. The extension is made by the developer of the WordByWord iPhone app. It has no account or sign-in, and it never asks for your name or email address.

1. What the extension sends, and when

Nothing is read from a web page or sent anywhere until you trigger a feature: pressing the activation key over a paragraph, pressing Control on a word, using the popup, or opening an entry in the side panel. When you do, the extension processes only the text involved.

  • Text you translate or look up. The paragraph, sentence or word you selected, together with the sentence around it as context, is sent to our server at api.word-by-word.app. Our server forwards it to the translation or AI provider needed for the request: Microsoft Azure Translator or Google Cloud Translation for translations, and OpenAI or Google Gemini for word meanings and structure analysis. The text is used only to produce the result and is not kept by our server after the request is answered. The providers process it under their own privacy policies.
  • On-device translation. If you choose Chrome's built-in translation model in the settings, paragraph translation happens inside your browser and the text is not sent to us or to a translation provider. Word meanings and structure analysis still use our server.
  • Request details. Each request to our server carries the extension version, a timestamp and a signed token that contains a random install ID (see section 3). It does not carry the address of the page you are reading, your browsing history, or anything that identifies you personally.

2. Usage statistics

By default the extension reports anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics 4 through Google's Measurement Protocol. These events say that the extension was installed or updated, which feature was used (paragraph translation, word meaning, structure analysis, quick translation, read-aloud), the language pair and translation service chosen, which setting was changed and its new value, that a daily limit was reached, and error types with their messages. Each event carries the extension version and the random install ID. No page addresses, no translated text and no looked-up words are included.

You can turn this off at any time with Allow anonymous usage statistics on the extension's options page. Google handles these events under Google's privacy policy. The extension does not set cookies.

3. Data stored in your browser

  • Settings (languages, hotkeys, appearance, feature switches) are stored with Chrome's sync storage. If Chrome sync is on, Google syncs them to your other browsers signed in with the same Google account.
  • Translation history, looked-up sentences and daily usage counters are stored with Chrome's local storage and stay on this computer. You can delete history entries in the side panel.
  • The install ID is a random identifier created when the extension is installed. It is stored locally, is not linked to your identity, and a reinstall creates a new one.

Removing the extension deletes all of this data from your browser.

4. Read-aloud

Read-aloud uses the text-to-speech voices available to Chrome. Voices provided by your operating system run on your device; if your browser offers a network voice, that voice's provider receives the text to speak it.

5. Permissions explained

  • storage: to keep your settings, history and usage counters as described in section 3.
  • tabs and activeTab: to find the tab you are working in and exchange messages with the extension's own script on that page. The extension does not read your tab list or history.
  • tts: to speak text with the browser's text-to-speech voices.
  • sidePanel: to show translation history and sentence details in Chrome's side panel.
  • Access to www.google-analytics.com: only for the usage statistics in section 2.

The extension's script is loaded on every web page so that it can respond when you trigger it. It does not read or send anything until you do.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address or any account information.
  • We do not collect your browsing history or the addresses of the pages you read.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties, other than the service providers named in sections 1 and 2, who act on our behalf.
  • We do not show advertisements.

7. Children's privacy

This extension is not intended for children under the age of 14. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly address the issue.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
